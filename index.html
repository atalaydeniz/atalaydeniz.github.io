About Me

Hey, I'm Deniz and this is my playground. I am mainly interested in Distributed Systems, both theoretical and practical parts. I want to build robust, secure and scalable applications for people to use in a variety of domains. Other than this, I like Algorithmics, Theory of Computation (Languages, Complexity Theory etc.) and Logic. Other than that, spending some time in Schwarzwald on Sundays makes me happy.

I've recently finished my Master's Degree in Freiburg. I wrote my thesis on Distributed Systems. Specifically, we designed a deterministic distributed Set Cover algorithm which improves the time complexity of the best-known logarithmic approximation. You can check it out.

Here, I want to create a blog to share mini software projects and write about some other stuff that I haven't determined yet. It will probably contain mathematics of some kind.

Lastly, the writings posted in this site is 0% AI-generated. Some (very small) parts of the software may contain AI-generated code.
